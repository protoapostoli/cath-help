## View Guest List

 - Once logged in, click the **Invites and RSVPs** button on the dashboard.
 - Locate your event in the table and click on the three dots on the far right hand side of it.
 - Click **View Guest List**

This will bring up your guest list for that particular event. You can then view who has responded, whether they're a YES, NO, or MAYBE, and who still has a pending invitation.
