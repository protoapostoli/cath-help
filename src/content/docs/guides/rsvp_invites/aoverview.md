Invites and RSVPs are essential for event planning. Information on them can be found over on the left.

Invite and RSVP Options:
 - Create Invitations
 - Upload images created from Canva or other sources
 - Send out Invitations via the built-in system
 - Collect RSVPs from your guests
 - View the Guest List
 - Send Email messages to your guests about your event
