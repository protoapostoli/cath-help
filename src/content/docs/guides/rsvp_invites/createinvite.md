---
title: Create Invitations
---

:::tip
Any user can create invitations to their own events.
:::

 - Once logged in, click the **Invites and RSVPs** button on the dashboard.
 - Click **+ New Invitation** on the top of the page.
 - Input the requested information.
   - Some of the information is optional, but all of it is useful!
   - Emails are accepted, one email per line in the text area.
 - Click Create Invite & Send Emails

:::tip
This will immediately send out email invitations to your event with the details you've submitted.
:::
