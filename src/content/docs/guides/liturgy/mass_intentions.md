title: Mass Intentions & Stipends
description: Booking Mass intentions, tracking donor stipends, setting intention limits, and publishing lists.
---

The **Mass Intentions** module (`/mass-intentions`) manages Mass intention requests, donor offerings, canon law compliance limits, and publishing reports for parish bulletins.

---

## Accessing Mass Intentions

- Navigate to **Liturgy** (or **Admin**) > **Mass Intentions** (`/mass-intentions`).
- Toggle between **List View** and **Calendar View** to see booked and available Mass dates.

---

## Booking a Mass Intention

1. Click **+ Book Mass Intention** (or click on an open Mass in the calendar view).
2. Complete the intention details:
   - **Mass Date & Time**: Choose from the available Mass schedule.
   - **Intended For**: Name of the individual or family.
   - **Status**: Indicate whether the person is **Deceased** (†) or **Living** (Special Intention / Healing / Thanksgiving).
   - **Offered By / Donor**: Name and contact details of the requester.
   - **Stipend Offering**: Standard diocesan Mass offering amount (e.g., $10 / $15) or custom amount.
   - **Payment Status**: Paid (Cash, Check, Online) or Pending.
3. Click **Save Intention**.

---

## Mass Intention Capacity & Limits

To follow Canon Law and diocesan guidelines regarding collective intentions:
- The system automatically enforces intention caps per Mass (e.g., maximum 1 intention per Mass, or allowed multi-intention days).
- Alerts staff if a donor exceeds parish frequency limits for a single person within a year.

---

## Bulletin & Sacristy Reports

- **Bulletin Export**: Generate formatted lists of upcoming Mass intentions for inclusion in weekly church bulletins.
- **Sacristy Binder Export**: Print daily and weekly lists of intentions with celebrant names for the altar and ambo binders.

---

## Configuring Mass Intention Settings

Parish administrators can customize policies at:
- **Settings** > **Mass Intentions** (`/settings/mass-intentions`).
- Set default stipend amounts, booking window opening dates (e.g., opening the annual Mass book on Nov 1), and intention caps per individual.
