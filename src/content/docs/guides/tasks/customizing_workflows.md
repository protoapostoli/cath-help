---
title: Customizing Task Boards & Workflows
description: Configuring board columns, workflows, work-in-progress limits, and team access.
---

Parishes can create tailored task boards with unique workflows for different departments and ministries.

---

## Creating a New Task Board

1. In the Task dashboard (`/tasks`), open the board selector dropdown.
2. Click **+ Create New Board**.
3. Set the board title (e.g., *Capital Campaign*, *Facilities Work Orders*, *Faith Formation*).
4. Choose whether the board is **Shared with All Staff** or restricted to specific team members.

---

## Configuring Board Columns & Steps

1. In the top right corner of the board, click **Board Settings** (`TaskBoardSettings`).
2. Manage columns:
   - **Add Columns**: Create custom workflow stages (e.g., *Backlog*, *Quotes Requested*, *Pastor Approval*, *Ordered*, *Completed*).
   - **Column Colors**: Assign distinct header colors for rapid visual scanning.
   - **Reorder**: Drag columns left or right to match your process flow.
   - **Archive/Delete**: Clean up completed or deprecated columns.

---

## Custom Labels & Tags

- Create board-specific colored labels (e.g., *HVAC*, *Landscaping*, *Electrical*, *Permits*).
- Filter the entire board by clicking any label.
