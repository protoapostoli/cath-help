The **Funeral Files** module (`/funeral-files`) helps parish staff, bereavement ministers, and priests organize Order of Christian Funerals liturgies with compassion and efficiency.

---

## Creating a Funeral File

1. Go to **Funeral Files** and click **+ New Funeral File** (`/funeral-files/create`).
2. Enter the initial details:
   - **Deceased Information**: Full name, date of birth, date of death, parishioner status.
   - **Primary Family Contact (Next of Kin)**: Name, relationship to deceased, phone, and email.
   - **Mortuary / Funeral Home**: Mortuary name, funeral director contact, and casket/urn details.
   - **Cemetery / Burial Site**: Location of the Graveside Committal service.
3. Click **Create Funeral File**.

---

## Coordinating the Funeral Services

Within the funeral file (`/funeral-files/{id}`), you can coordinate all three stations of the Catholic funeral rite:

### 1. Multi-Stage Liturgy Scheduling
- **Vigil / Rosary Service**: Date, time, location (Church, Chapel, or Mortuary), and leader of prayer.
- **Funeral Mass / Liturgy**: Date, time, church location, and presiding celebrant/concelebrants/deacons.
- **Rite of Committal**: Date, time, cemetery location, and presider.
- **Reception**: Hall booking coordination for the post-funeral parish reception.

### 2. Liturgy Planning & Family Selections
- **Scripture Readings**: First Reading (Old Testament), Responsorial Psalm, Second Reading (New Testament), Gospel.
- **Music & Hymns**: Entrance hymn, Offertory, Communion, Song of Farewell, and Recessional.
- **Liturgical Ministers**: Family readers, gift bearers, pallbearers, and altar servers.
- **Words of Remembrance**: Speaker name and parish guidelines compliance.

### 3. Requirements Checklist
- Track the diocesan funeral fee schedule, cremation permission documents (if applicable), and memorial bulletin/program drafts.

### 4. Financial Ledger
- Manage church fees, organist/cantor fees, and altar server stipends.
- Log payments made directly by the family or invoiced to the funeral home/mortuary.

## Customizing Funeral Requirements

Configure default checklist items at:
- **Settings** > **Funeral Requirements** (`/settings/funeral-requirements`).
