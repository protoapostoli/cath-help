The **Quinceañera Files** module (`/quinceanera-files`) coordinates thanksgiving Masses and blessings for young women celebrating their fifteenth birthday.

---

## Creating a Quinceañera File

1. Navigate to **Quinceañera Files** and click **+ New Quinceañera File** (`/quinceanera-files/create`).
2. Input the basic details:
   - **Candidate Name**: First and last name, date of birth.
   - **Parents Information**: Contact phone numbers, email, and parish registration status.
   - **Event Information**: Requested date, time, church/chapel location, and presiding clergy.
   - **Service Type**: Mass of Thanksgiving or Blessing ceremony.
3. Click **Create Quinceañera File**.

---

## Managing the Quinceañera File

Within the individual file (`/quinceanera-files/{id}`):

### 1. Requirements Checklist
- **Sacramental Verification**: Baptism, First Holy Communion, and Confirmation certificates.
- **Formation & Retreats**: Attendance at youth ministry sessions, Quinceañera preparation retreat, or community service hours.
- **Rehearsal Scheduling**: Date and time for court of honor rehearsal.

### 2. Court of Honor & Liturgy Planning
- Record details for the Damas, Chambelanes, and padrinos/godparents.
- Track readings, intentions, and traditional presentation gifts (Bible, Rosary, Crown, Flowers for Our Lady).

### 3. Financial Ledger & Fee Templates
- Track parish facility usage fees, musicians, and security/cleaning deposits.
- Log deposits, balance payments, and issue receipts.

---

## Quinceañera Requirement Settings

Customize the checklist defaults anytime at:
- **Settings** > **Quinceañera Requirements** (`/settings/quinceanera-requirements`).
