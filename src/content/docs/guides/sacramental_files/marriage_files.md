The **Marriage Files** module (`/marriage-files`) allows parish staff and clergy to manage couples preparing for the Sacrament of Holy Matrimony, from initial inquiry through the wedding liturgy and recording in the sacramental register.

---

## Accessing Marriage Files

- In the top navigation, click **Sacraments** (or **Files**) and select **Marriage Files**.
- The main directory displays all active, completed, and archived marriage cases with couple names, wedding dates, presiders, and checklist completion percentages.

---

## Creating a New Marriage File

1. Navigate to **Marriage Files** and click **+ New Marriage File** (or visit `/marriage-files/create`).
2. Enter the initial intake information:
   - **Bride & Groom Names**: First and last names.
   - **Contact Details**: Email addresses, phone numbers, and home addresses.
   - **Tentative Wedding Date & Time**: Set the requested ceremony date and location (Main Church, Chapel, etc.).
   - **Liturgy Type**: Mass or Ceremony outside of Mass.
   - **Language**: English, Spanish, Bilingual, etc.
3. Click **Create Marriage File**.

---

## Managing the Marriage File

Click on any couple from the index to open their dedicated file (`/marriage-files/{id}`).

### 1. Couple & Case Details
- Update bride and groom personal information, dates of birth, and parishioner status.
- Assign the **Celebrant** (Priest or Deacon) and Concelebrants.
- Schedule the **Wedding Rehearsal** date and time.

### 2. Requirements & Canonical Checklist
The file tracks all necessary documentation required by Canon Law and your diocese:
- **Baptismal Certificates** with recent notations (issued within 6 months).
- **Prenuptial Questionnaire (Form A)** and Witness Affidavits (Form B).
- **Pre-Cana / Marriage Preparation Retreat** certificate.
- **FOCCUS / Prepare-Enrich Inventory** completion.
- **Civil Marriage License** verification.
- **Canonical Dispensations / Permissions** (e.g., Disparity of Cult, Mixed Religion, Canonical Form).

:::tip Marking Requirements
Click on any requirement item to update its status to **Completed**, upload a scanned PDF/image attachment, or add an explanatory note.
:::

### 3. Financial Ledger & Payments
- Manage parish fees (Church donation, priest stipend, cantor, organist, sound technician).
- Log incoming deposits and final balance payments.
- Print an itemized **Wedding Financial Statement** or receipt for the couple.

### 4. Liturgy & Readings Planning
- Plan the liturgical readings (First Reading, Responsorial Psalm, Second Reading, Gospel).
- Record musical selections, unity candle / lasso / arras traditions, and bridal party details.

### 5. Notes & @Mentions
- Maintain a running chronological history of communications, meetings, and pastoral observations.
- Tag staff members (e.g., `@FatherJohn Check the dispensation status with the Chancery`) to send instant in-app alerts.

---

## Customizing Marriage Requirements

Parish Administrators can configure default requirement templates:
1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Marriage Requirements** (`/settings/marriage-requirements`).
2. Add, reorder, or remove standard checklist items that will automatically apply to all newly created marriage files.
