The **Facility Rental Files** module (`/rental-files`) tracks non-liturgical bookings of parish social halls, gymnasiums, meeting rooms, and outdoor facilities.

---

## Creating a Rental File

1. Navigate to **Rental Files** (`/rental-files`) and click **+ New Rental File** (`/rental-files/create`).
2. Input the event details:
   - **Renter / Organization Name**: Contact person, phone, email, and address.
   - **Event Type**: Reception, Anniversary, Meeting, Fundraiser, Community Event.
   - **Facility Requested**: Parish Hall, Kitchen, Pavilion, Meeting Rooms.
   - **Event Date & Time**: Setup start, event start/end, cleanup conclusion.
3. Click **Create Rental File**.

---

## Managing Rental Compliance & Details

Inside the rental file (`/rental-files/{id}`):

### 1. Requirements & Compliance Checklist
- **Signed Rental Contract / Agreement**: Upload scanned signed agreement.
- **Diocesan Special Events Insurance**: Certificate of Liability Insurance or diocesan event insurance policy.
- **Alcohol Policy & Permits**: Security officer requirements, liquor license/permits (if serving alcohol).
- **Setup & Floor Plan**: Room configuration, table/chair counts, kitchen usage terms.

### 2. Financial Ledger & Security Deposits
- **Rental Fee**: Facility usage charge based on hours or package.
- **Refundable Security/Cleaning Deposit**: Track deposit received and track return/deductions post-event.
- **Janitorial / Sound Tech Fees**: Itemize staffing charges.
- Record payments and print official receipts and balance statements.

---

## Customizing Rental Requirements

Configure default rental requirements at:
- **Settings** > **Rental Requirements** (`/settings/rental-requirements`).
