---
title: Care Facilities & Group Residences
description: Managing hospitals, nursing homes, and assisted living centers within the parish boundaries.
---

The **Care Facilities Manager** (`/pastoral-care/facilities`) enables parishes to maintain an organized directory of all hospitals, convalescent homes, hospice houses, and memory care communities within their pastoral care territory.

---

## Managing Care Facilities

1. Navigate to **Pastoral Care** > **Care Facilities** (`/pastoral-care/facilities`).
2. Click **+ Add Care Facility**.
3. Provide facility information:
   - **Facility Name**: (e.g., *St. Jude Senior Living*, *Community General Hospital*).
   - **Facility Type**: Hospital, Skilled Nursing, Assisted Living, Memory Care, Hospice.
   - **Address & Main Phone**: Physical address and nurse station / front desk phone.
   - **Chaplaincy / Pastoral Contact**: On-site chaplain or activities director contact.
   - **Entry / Check-In Instructions**: COVID/health protocols, badges, visitor sign-in location, code to chapel/sacristy.

---

## Facility Resident Mapping

- Within each Care Facility card, view a live list of all registered parish homebound members currently residing in that facility, along with their room and bed numbers.
- Streamlines group Communion services, First Friday Mass visitations, and multi-patient priest anointings.
