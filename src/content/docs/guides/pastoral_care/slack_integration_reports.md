CatholicOffice provides instant team communication via Slack and comprehensive statistical reporting on all pastoral care visits.

---

## Slack Integration for Urgent Sick Calls

Parishes can connect their team's Slack workspace to receive real-time notifications when sick call requests are logged.

### Setting Up Slack:
1. Navigate to **Pastoral Care** > **Settings** (`/pastoral-care/settings`).
2. Click **Connect Slack** (`/pastoral-care/slack/authorize`).
3. Authorize CatholicOffice in your Slack workspace and select the desired channel (e.g., `#clergy-sick-calls` or `#pastoral-care`).
4. Once connected:
   - **Urgent Sick Calls** will post an immediate alert with patient name, location, and a direct link to the sick call card.
   - Updates to assignment status (e.g., when a priest claims the call) will update in the thread.

---

## Pastoral Care Statistics & Reports

1. Go to **Pastoral Care** > **Reports** (`/pastoral-care/report`).
2. Filter by date range (Month, Quarter, Year-to-Date):
   - **Total Sick Calls Logged** vs. Completed.
   - **Breakdown by Sacrament**: Anointings, Confessions, Viaticum.
   - **Homebound Visits Logged**: Total visits conducted across homebound parishioners and nursing facilities.
   - **Minister Activity**: Distribution of visits across priests, deacons, and EMHCs.
3. Export reports for parish council meetings, annual archdiocesan pastoral reports, or ministry reviews.
