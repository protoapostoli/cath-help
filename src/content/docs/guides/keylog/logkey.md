---
title: Check In/Out Keys
---

:::tip
Only Receptionists and account admins, calendar admins, and those with explicit permissions can check keys in and out for parishioners.
:::

 - Once logged in, click the **Key Log** button on the dashboard.
 - Click on the key you need to check out.
 - Fill out the form on the top right of the key log.
   - Be sure to include identify information like the person's name and email/phone number.
   - Optionally, enter the reason they're taking the key.
 - Click **Check Out Key**

To Check the key back in, follow the steps above, but then click **Check In Key**.
