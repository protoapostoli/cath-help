---
title: Approval Calendar
description: Reviewing pending events in visual context, checking room conflicts, and approving or denying requests.
---

The **Approval Calendar** (`/calendar/view/approval`) provides ministry leaders and Calendar Administrators with a visual layout to review pending event requests alongside existing bookings.

---

## Accessing the Approval Calendar

- Navigate to **Calendar** > **Approval Calendar** (`/calendar/view/approval`).
- The calendar highlights pending event requests in amber/yellow against approved events in the parish calendar.

---

## Reviewing & Resolving Conflicts

When reviewing an event in the Approval Calendar:
1. Click on any pending event box.
2. Review the requested dates, setup/cleanup buffers, assigned rooms, and ministry details.
3. Check for any visual room or resource overlaps with already approved events.
4. Take action:
   - **Approve**: Immediately confirms the reservation and places it on the live parish calendar.
   - **Decline / Deny**: Rejects the request with an optional note sent to the requester explaining why.
   - **Edit Before Approval**: Adjust times or switch rooms before approving.

---

## Two-Tier Approval System

- **Ministry Approval**: Ministry leaders can first review and endorse requests submitted by their ministry members.
- **Calendar Admin Final Approval**: The primary Calendar Administrator reviews the endorsed request and gives final authorization.
