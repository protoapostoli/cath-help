The **Reception & Front Desk** module (`/reception`) provides staff and volunteers with rapid tools for managing visitors, caller inquiries, message routing, and front office logistics.

---

## Key Reception Tools

### 1. Front Desk Event Log
- Record incoming calls, messages, Mass intention inquiries, and drop-in visitors.
- Tag staff members with `@Name` to deliver immediate notifications.
- Categorize event logs by type for fast reporting and staff handoffs.

### 2. Reception Valet Mode (`/reception/valet`)
- A streamlined, distraction-free interface optimized for front desk tablets and reception kiosks.
- Quick buttons to log visitors, lookup room reservations, and submit urgent sick calls.

### 3. Key Log & Visitor Badges
- Quick access to the **Key Log** to check out room and facility keys to ministry leaders and vendors.
