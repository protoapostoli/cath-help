Parish Administrators can configure granular, role-based permissions for staff members, clergy, and ministry volunteers.

---

## Modifying User Permissions

1. Click your parish name in the top navigation bar.
2. Select **Member Permissions** (`/permissions`).
3. Locate the user you wish to edit and click **Edit Permissions** (`/permissions/edit/{id}`).
4. Toggle the specific feature permissions below and click **Save Changes**.

---

## Available Permission Matrix

### 1. Calendar & Scheduling
- **Can Request Events**: Allows submitting event and room booking requests.
- **Can Approve Events**: Grants approval authorization for pending calendar requests.
- **Can Create / Modify Events**: Direct edit and bypass permissions for calendar bookings.
- **Can Create Ministries**: Ability to register new parish ministries and leadership rosters.

### 2. Liturgy & Presider Scheduling
- **Can Access Presider Schedule**: View-only access to liturgical presider assignments.
- **Can Manage Presider Schedule**: Assign priests, deacons, and homilists across parish Masses.

### 3. Sacramental & Event Files
- **Can Access Marriage Files**: Full management of marriage intake, canonical files, and weddings.
- **Can Access Baptism Files**: Manage infant/child baptisms and review online public registrations.
- **Can Access Quinceañera Files**: Manage Quinceañera candidates, requirements, and liturgies.
- **Can Access Funeral Files**: Coordinate mortuary contacts, family planning, and Order of Christian Funerals liturgies.
- **Can Access Rental Files**: Manage parish hall bookings, contracts, and security deposits.

### 4. Pastoral Care & Homebound Ministry
- **Can Manage Pastoral Care**: Access sick calls, emergency triage, and care facility directories.
- **Can Manage Homebound**: Manage homebound parishioner directory and view contact details.
- **Is EMHC (Extraordinary Minister of Holy Communion)**: Assign to homebound visits and receive EMHC notifications.

### 5. Parish Tasks & Administration
- **Can Manage Tasks**: Create, configure, and assign parish task boards and columns.
- **Can Manage Keys**: Check-in, check-out, and audit physical parish facility keys.
- **Reception Access**: Access front desk event logging and valet reception tools.
- **Can Set Permissions**: Grants ability to invite new staff and modify permissions of other users.
