---
title: Fee Schedule Templates
description: Setting up parish-wide fee structures and default amounts for sacraments and facilities.
---

The **Fee Schedule Templates** settings (`/settings/fee-templates`) allow parish administrators to define standardized fee items and amounts that automatically apply when creating new sacramental or event files.

---

## Managing Fee Templates

:::note[Admin Only]
Only Team Admins or users with permission to manage parish settings can configure fee templates.
:::

1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Fee Templates** (`/settings/fee-templates`).
2. Click **+ New Fee Template**.
3. Choose the appropriate category:
   - **Marriage Preparation** (e.g., Church Usage Fee, Priest Honorarium, Organist, Cantor, Sound Tech)
   - **Baptism Preparation** (e.g., Sacramental Offering, Certificate & Candle Fee)
   - **Funeral Service** (e.g., Church Liturgy Fee, Musician, Altar Server Stipends)
   - **Quinceañera** (e.g., Liturgy Fee, Rehearsal Coordinator, Security Deposit)
   - **Facility / Room Rental** (e.g., Hourly Hall Rate, Kitchen Fee, Refundable Cleaning Deposit)
   - **General**
4. Enter the fee name and standard default dollar amount.
5. Click **Save Fee Template**.

---

## Applying Templates to Files

When staff members create or manage any sacramental or rental file, they can populate fees directly from these predefined templates with a single click, eliminating manual pricing errors.
