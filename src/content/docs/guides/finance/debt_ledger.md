title: Accounts Receivable & Debt Ledger
description: Centralized tracking of parish fees, deposits, balance payments, and financial statements.
---

The **Accounts Receivable Ledger** (`/accounts-receivable`) consolidates all outstanding fees, payments, and balances across every event and sacramental file in one centralized dashboard.

---

## Accessing the Debt Ledger

- In the navigation bar, click **Finance** > **Accounts Receivable** (`/accounts-receivable`).
- The ledger displays all active accounts categorized across:
  - **Marriage Files**
  - **Baptism Files**
  - **Quinceañera Files**
  - **Funeral Files**
  - **Facility Rentals**

---

## Key Features

### 1. Unified Balance Overview
- Instantly view **Total Fees Assessed**, **Total Payments Collected**, and **Outstanding Balance Due** for each case.
- Search and filter by family name, case ID, date, or file type.

### 2. Itemized Ledger Breakdown
- Click to expand any account row to view the full itemized ledger:
  - Itemized fees (Church offering, Celebrant stipend, Musician/Cantor, Security deposit, Janitorial).
  - Chronological transaction log of all recorded payments, including payment method (Cash, Check, Credit Card, Online), receipt numbers, and notes.

### 3. Recording Payments & Issuing Receipts
- Record incoming payments directly into the ledger:
  - Specify payment method, amount, and date.
  - Add reference details (e.g., Check #1042).
- Automatically updates remaining balances in real time.

### 4. Official Financial Statements
- Generate and download PDF **Financial Statements** (`/financial/statement/{type}/{id}`) to provide families with official itemized billing and receipts for tax and personal records.
