title: Calendly & Parish Appointments
description: Integrating Calendly OAuth, syncing parish staff booking links, and viewing appointment schedules.
---

The **Appointments** integration (`/appointments`) links Calendly accounts to CatholicOffice, allowing parishioners and couples to schedule meetings with priests and parish staff while avoiding double-bookings.

---

## Connecting Calendly

1. Navigate to **Appointments** or visit `/calendly`.
2. Click **Connect with Calendly**.
3. Authorize CatholicOffice to access your Calendly account.
4. CatholicOffice automatically registers secure webhooks to sync bookings in real time.

---

## Appointments Dashboard

- View all upcoming scheduled appointments across parish staff and clergy (`/appointments`).
- Syncs event invitee details, questionnaire answers, and meeting locations directly into the parish office workflow.
