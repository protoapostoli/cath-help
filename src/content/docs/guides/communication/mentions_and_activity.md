CatholicOffice provides centralized team communication tools so staff never miss important notes, task assignments, or sacramental updates.

---

## Using @Mentions Anywhere

You can tag colleagues anywhere notes or comments are supported across CatholicOffice:
- Sacramental & Event Files (Marriage, Baptism, Quinceañera, Funeral, Rentals)
- Reception Event Log & Front Desk Notes
- Parish Tasks Comments & Descriptions
- Sick Call Notes & Homebound logs

### How to Mention:
Type `@` followed by any staff member's name (e.g., `@FatherJoe`, `@Sarah`). As you type, an autocomplete dropdown will appear.

---

## Unified Mentions Dashboard (`/my-mentions`)

Whenever someone mentions you in a note:
1. You receive an in-app notification and email alert.
2. The notification is logged in your **Unified Mentions Dashboard** (`/my-mentions`).
3. Click on any mention in your inbox to jump directly to the exact note or record via the smart **Mention Bridge** (`/mentions/{id}/view`).
4. Filter mentions by **Unread**, **Read**, parish team, or date range, and click **Mark All as Read** when caught up.

---

## Global Activity Audit Trail (`/activity`)

The **Activity Dashboard** (`/activity`) gives pastors and administrators a chronological, team-wide audit log of all actions taken in the parish workspace:
- Events created, edited, approved, or deleted.
- Sacramental files updated or status changes.
- Payments and fees logged into accounts receivable.
- Sick calls received and triaged.
- Filter by staff user, date range, or action type for accountability and peace of mind.
