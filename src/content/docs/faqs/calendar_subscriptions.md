---
title: How do I sync parish events to my iPhone, Google Calendar, or Outlook?
description: Subscribing to live iCal / WebCal calendar feeds for parish and presider events.
---

You can subscribe to live CatholicOffice calendar feeds so parish events, ministry meetings, or priest presider schedules update automatically on your phone or personal calendar app.

---

## Subscribing to Calendar Feeds

1. In the navigation bar, go to **Calendar** > **Subscribe** (`/calendar/subscribe`).
2. Choose your subscription feed:
   - **Full Parish Calendar**: All approved parish events.
   - **Specific Ministry**: Only events belonging to a particular ministry (e.g., *Youth Ministry*, *Knights of Columbus*).
   - **Presider Schedule**: Liturgical Mass assignments for priests and deacons.
3. Click **Copy Subscription URL** (or click the **Subscribe via Apple Calendar / Google Calendar** button).

---

## Adding to Your Device

- **Apple Calendar (iOS / Mac)**: Open Calendar > **File** > **New Calendar Subscription** > Paste the URL > Set Auto-refresh to *Every Hour*.
- **Google Calendar**: On the web, click **Other calendars (+)** > **From URL** > Paste the URL.
- **Microsoft Outlook**: Go to Calendar > **Add Calendar** > **Subscribe from web** > Paste the URL.
