---
title: What happens when an urgent sick call is submitted?
description: Understanding emergency triage notifications, priest dispatch, and printable emergency cards.
---

When a caller reports a sick call or emergency Viaticum request, CatholicOffice ensures rapid dispatch:

---

## 1. Priority Triage
- **Urgent / Imminent Danger of Death**: Triggers high-priority SMS and email alerts to all on-call clergy and pastoral administrators.
- **Slack Alert**: If your parish has connected Slack (`/pastoral-care/settings`), an urgent notification card is instantly posted to your designated clergy Slack channel.
- **Routine / Homebound**: Routes to the Homebound Coordinator or assigned EMHC volunteer.

---

## 2. Priest Emergency Card
Before leaving for the hospital or home, the responding priest can click **Print Sick Call Card** (`/pastoral-care/sick-calls/{id}/print`) to take a physical summary sheet containing:
- Patient name, medical location, room number, gate codes.
- Family contact numbers and requested sacraments (Anointing, Confession, Communion).
