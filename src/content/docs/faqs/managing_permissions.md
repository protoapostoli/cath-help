---

## Modifying Permissions

1. Click your parish name at the top of CatholicOffice.
2. Select **Member Permissions** (`/permissions`).
3. Click **Edit** next to the user.
4. Toggle specific module permissions:
   - **Calendar Approval**: Approve or decline room requests.
   - **Sacramental Files**: Marriage, Baptism, Quinceañera, Funeral, or Rental files access.
   - **Pastoral Care**: Access sick calls, homebound rosters, and facility records.
   - **Finance**: View and log payments in the Debt Ledger.
   - **Presider Schedule**: Edit or view liturgical clergy assignments.
5. Click **Save Changes**.
