---
title: How do I track payments and issue receipts for weddings and rentals?
description: Logging deposits, balance payments, and printing official financial statements in the Debt Ledger.
---

CatholicOffice automatically connects all sacramental preparations and facility bookings to a centralized financial ledger.

---

## 1. Within an Individual File
When viewing any Marriage, Baptism, Quinceañera, Funeral, or Rental file:
1. Scroll to the **Financial Ledger** section.
2. Click **+ Add Payment** to record cash, checks, or online payments.
3. The remaining balance updates automatically.
4. Click **Print Statement** (`/financial/statement/{type}/{id}`) to generate an official itemized receipt for the family.

---

## 2. From the Central Accounts Receivable Ledger
1. Navigate to **Finance** > **Accounts Receivable** (`/accounts-receivable`).
2. Search by family name or filter by file type to view all outstanding parish balances.
3. Expand any row to review payment logs or add new payments on the spot.
