---
title: How do I book a Mass Intention and export it for the bulletin?
description: Booking living/deceased intentions, logging stipends, and generating bulletin announcement lists.
---

The **Mass Intentions** module (`/mass-intentions`) allows front desk staff to record Mass intentions quickly:

---

## 1. Booking an Intention
1. Go to **Liturgy** > **Mass Intentions** (`/mass-intentions`).
2. Click **+ Book Mass Intention**.
3. Select the Mass date/time, enter the intended name, living/deceased (†) status, and donor info.
4. Record the stipend offering (Cash, Check, Online) and click **Save Intention**.

---

## 2. Generating Bulletin & Ambo Lists
- Click **Export Bulletin List** to generate a formatted text or CSV export of all intentions for the upcoming week, ready to copy into your church bulletin publication.
