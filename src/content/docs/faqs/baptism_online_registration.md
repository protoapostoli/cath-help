---
title: How do parents register online for Baptism?
description: Sharing token-gated online baptism registration links with inquiring families.
---

CatholicOffice provides each parish with a secure, self-service **Public Baptism Registration URL** to share with parents inquiring about infant or child baptism.

---

## Getting Your Parish Registration Link

1. Go to **Baptism Files** (`/baptism-files`) or **Settings** > **Baptism Requirements**.
2. Click **Copy Public Registration Link** to copy your parish's unique private registration URL.
3. Place this link on your parish website's Sacraments page, church bulletin, or email it directly to parents.

---

## What Parents See & Submit

- **Child Information**: Name, DOB, birth city, and digital upload of State Birth Certificate.
- **Parents & Godparents**: Contact details, church attendance, and Catholic marriage status.
- **Automated Routing**: Once submitted, the system creates a new **Baptism File** in your parish dashboard and alerts parish staff.
